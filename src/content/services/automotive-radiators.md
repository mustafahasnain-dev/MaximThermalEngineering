## Tractor radiators in harvest season

Tractors work in chaff and dust for long hours at harvest time, often towing heavy implements in the hottest months. [verify] A radiator that is only partly blocked can push the engine over its limit. Check and clean the radiator screen and core every day during harvest.

## What to tell us

1. Vehicle type: car, three-wheeler or tractor
2. Make, model and year
3. Engine type, if known
4. A photo of the old radiator, if you have it
