## What makes heavy machinery cooling different?

Heavy machines run at high load in dust and heat, often for long shifts, with little time for cleaning. [verify] Their cooling package usually combines several cores in a small space, so a blockage in one core heats the air reaching the next. That is why a machine can overheat even when the engine radiator itself looks clean.

## Signs a machine's cooling package needs attention

1. Engine or hydraulic temperature climbs faster than it used to under the same work.
2. The machine derates or alarms in the afternoon heat.
3. Coolant or oil is being topped up with no visible leak on the engine.
4. Fins are bent, packed with dust or coated in oil.

Each machine type has its own page with the cooling problems we see on it.
