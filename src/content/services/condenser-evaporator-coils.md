## What to measure on an old coil

1. Finned length, height and depth
2. Number of rows and tubes per row
3. Fin spacing (fins per inch) [verify]
4. Number of circuits and the position and size of the connections
5. Tube diameter and fin material

Photos of both connection ends and the nameplate of the unit help us match the coil.

## Signs a coil is failing

- Cooling capacity drops even after the coil is cleaned
- Refrigerant needs topping up regularly
- White corrosion or flaking on the fins
- Oil stains on the coil, which can mark a refrigerant leak [verify]
