## How to compare heat exchanger types

| Type | Best for | Needs cooling water? | Typical use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shell and tube | Liquid to liquid, higher pressures | Usually | Oil coolers, process coolers, heaters |
| Air cooled | Rejecting heat to air | No | Remote sites, water-short plants |
| Fin tube | Heating or cooling air or gas | No | Dryers, air heaters, gas coolers |
| Waste heat recovery | Capturing heat from exhaust or hot streams | Depends on use | Engine exhaust, furnace flue gas |

[verify] This table is a general guide. The final choice depends on your fluids, pressures, fouling and site.

## Why plants replace heat exchangers

The most common reason is that the duty has changed: the plant runs more product, a hotter process or a different fluid. The second is fouling and corrosion that cleaning can no longer fix. In both cases we check the old design against today's conditions before quoting a copy.
