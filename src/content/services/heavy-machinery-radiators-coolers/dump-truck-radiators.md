## Planning radiator work for a truck fleet

When a fleet runs the same truck model, radiators tend to wear out at a similar age. Recording coolant temperatures on the hardest haul route and recoring the worst units first helps avoid several trucks failing in the same summer. [verify]

## What wears a dump truck radiator out

1. Dust and mud thrown up by the wheels and packed into the core
2. Vibration and frame flex on rough haul roads, cracking joints and mounts
3. Scale from topping up with untreated water
4. Damage from stones and debris hitting the core
