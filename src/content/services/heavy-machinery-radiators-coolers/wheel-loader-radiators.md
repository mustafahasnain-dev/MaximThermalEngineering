## Which circuit is overheating?

| Warning | Circuit to check first |
|---|---|
| Engine coolant temperature high | Engine radiator and fan |
| Transmission or converter temperature high | Transmission oil cooler |
| Hydraulic oil temperature high, slow lifting | Hydraulic oil cooler |
| Loss of power, black smoke | Charge air cooler [verify] |

[verify] These are the usual first checks; we confirm the fault by inspection.

## Why loaders suffer more than other machines

A loader works in the dust it raises, drives back and forth through the same material, and often reverses into the wind of its own dust cloud. [verify] The rear-mounted package pulls this dust straight onto the core faces.
