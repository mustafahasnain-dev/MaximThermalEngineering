## Why excavators overheat on Pakistani sites

An excavator draws air through its cooling stack right next to the dust it creates. Fine dust and oil mist build up between the radiator and the oil cooler, where the operator cannot see or reach them. [verify] In summer the air entering the stack is already hot, so the margin left after a partial blockage disappears.

## Daily checks an operator can make

1. Look through the side door at both faces of the stack for packed dust.
2. Check coolant level in the expansion tank when the engine is cold.
3. Watch hydraulic oil temperature on the display during the hottest hours.
4. Report any oil or coolant on the stack straight away.
