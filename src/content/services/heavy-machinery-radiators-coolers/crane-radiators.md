## What makes crane cooling different

A crane may stand still for long periods with the engine idling, then run the hydraulics hard during a lift. [verify] Its cooler sees short bursts of high heat rather than steady load, and with little road speed it relies fully on the fan for airflow. Keeping the fan and cooler face clean matters more than on a machine that travels.

## Before you call

1. Note which temperature rises: engine coolant, hydraulic oil or transmission oil.
2. Note when it happens: during travel, at idle or during lifts.
3. Photograph the radiator, cooler and any leaks.
