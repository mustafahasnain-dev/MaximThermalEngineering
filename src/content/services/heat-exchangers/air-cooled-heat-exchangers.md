## How ambient air temperature drives the design

An air cooler can only cool the fluid to some degrees above the air temperature. [verify] The closer you want the outlet to the air temperature, the more surface and fan power it needs. That is why the design air temperature is the most important number on an air cooler data sheet, and why units designed for cooler climates often fall short in a Pakistani summer.

## Keeping an air cooler at full capacity

1. Clean fins on a fixed schedule and after dust storms.
2. Check fan belts, blade pitch and motor current.
3. Watch the outlet temperature trend through the year.
4. Keep the area around the unit clear so hot air is not drawn back in.
