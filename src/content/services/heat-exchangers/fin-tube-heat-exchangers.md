## Where fin tube heat exchangers are used

Fins add surface on the air side, where heat transfer is weakest, so the exchanger can be much smaller than a bare tube design. [verify] This makes fin tube coils the usual choice wherever air or gas must be heated or cooled by a liquid or steam.

| Application | Medium in the tubes | Typical problem |
|---|---|---|
| Textile dryer and stenter | Steam or thermal oil | Lint blocking the fins |
| Food oven or drying line | Steam or hot water | Grease and dust on fins |
| Combustion air preheater | Hot water or exhaust heat | Corrosion at low temperature [verify] |
| Gas cooler | Water | Fouling inside the tubes |
