## What decides if heat recovery pays back

1. **Running hours:** an engine running most of the year gives far more heat than a standby set.
2. **Matching demand:** the plant must need the heat while the engine is running.
3. **Temperature:** hotter sources are easier and cheaper to recover from. [verify]
4. **Distance:** the heat must be used close to the source to avoid long insulated pipe runs.

## What we will not do

We will not quote fuel savings or payback figures before your heat source and demand have been measured. Any figure we give will be an engineer's estimate, marked as such.
