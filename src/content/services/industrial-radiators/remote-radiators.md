## How a remote radiator system works

Coolant leaves the engine, travels through pipework to the radiator outside, is cooled by air pushed through the core by electric fans, and returns to the engine. An expansion tank at the highest point keeps the system full and lets air escape. [verify]

## Design points we check on every remote radiator

1. Pipe size and length, so the engine's coolant pump can still deliver full flow
2. Height of the radiator above the engine and the position of the expansion tank
3. Hot air recirculation: the discharged air must not be drawn back into the core
4. Fan noise at the site boundary [verify]
5. Access for cleaning the core and servicing the fans
