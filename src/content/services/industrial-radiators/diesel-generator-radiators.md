faqs:
  - q: Why does my diesel generator overheat at full load but not at half load?
    a: Because at full load the engine rejects much more heat, and a partly blocked or undersized radiator can only cope with the lower load. [verify] Dirt on the fins, scale inside the tubes and a slipping fan belt are the usual causes.
  - q: Can you make a radiator for a CAT, Perkins or Cummins generator?

  - q: Should I recore my generator radiator or buy a new one?
    a: Recore it if the top and bottom tanks and the frame are sound and only the core is worn. Choose a new radiator if the tanks are cracked or corroded, or if the generator overheated even when the radiator was new.
  - q: How often should a generator radiator be cleaned?
    a: Clean the outside of the core whenever dust or oil builds up between the fins, and check it at every generator service. [verify] Sites near cement plants, roads or cotton mills often need more frequent cleaning.
  - q: Does a standby generator radiator need maintenance if it rarely runs?
    a: Yes. Coolant still ages and can corrode the core while the set stands idle, so check coolant condition and inspect the radiator at each scheduled service. [verify]

## What size of radiator does a diesel generator need?

The radiator must reject all the heat the engine puts into its jacket water at full rated load, on the hottest day the site sees. The engine maker's data sheet gives this heat rejection figure for each rating. [verify] In Pakistan, summer air temperatures above 45°C are common in Sindh and Punjab, so a radiator that was sized for a cooler climate can be too small here. [verify]

## Common diesel generator radiator problems

| Problem | Usual sign | Typical fix |
|---|---|---|
| Fins blocked by dust and oil | Temperature rises slowly over weeks | Deep clean the core |
| Scale inside the tubes | Hot spots, poor cooling even when clean | Chemical clean or recore |
| Cracked tank or header | Coolant loss, visible leak | Tank repair or replacement |
| Vibration cracks at mounts | Leak near brackets | Repair and new mounts |

[verify] The fixes above are typical; we confirm after inspection.
