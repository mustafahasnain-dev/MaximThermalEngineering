## Why does an industrial radiator fail?

Most industrial radiators fail because the core is blocked, outside by dust and oil or inside by scale, and it can no longer move enough heat. Other common causes are cracked tanks, failed joints from vibration, and corrosion from untreated coolant. [verify] The first sign is usually the engine running hotter than normal at full load before any visible leak appears.

## New radiator or rebuild?

A rebuild is usually the better choice when the tanks and frame are sound and only the core is worn. A new radiator makes more sense when the duty has changed, for example after an engine upgrade, or when the old design was undersized for the site. Our [radiator repair and refurbishment](/services/radiator-repair-refurbishment/) page explains how we decide.
