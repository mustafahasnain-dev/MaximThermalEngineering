## When one contractor saves time

Equipment installations stall when the civil foundation is late, the piping does not match the nozzles, or cables arrive after the equipment. Having mechanical, piping, electrical and civil work under one team means these interfaces are planned together. [verify]

1. Foundations and civil works
2. Equipment setting and alignment
3. Piping and connections
4. Electrical power and control
5. Testing, commissioning and handover
