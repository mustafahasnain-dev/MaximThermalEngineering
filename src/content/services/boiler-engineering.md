## Planning a boiler shutdown

1. Share the boiler's records and last inspection report.
2. Agree the scope after our inspection: tubes, refractory, burner, fittings. [verify]
3. Order long-lead materials such as tubes before the shutdown starts.
4. Fix the start-up and test dates with the plant's production plan.

## Safety first

Steam boilers are pressure equipment. Repairs to pressure parts must follow the applicable regulations and inspection requirements in Pakistan. [verify] We confirm these with you before work starts.
