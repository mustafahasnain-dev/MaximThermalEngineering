## Choosing a solar system type

| Your situation | Usually suits |
|---|---|
| Reliable grid, want lower bills | On-grid |
| No grid, or very unreliable grid | Off-grid with batteries |
| Grid with frequent outages | Hybrid |
| Generator-powered site | Hybrid with generator integration [verify] |

## What to send for a solar quote

1. Twelve months of electricity bills
2. Roof or land area, with photos
3. Generator sizes and running hours, if any
4. Whether you need backup during outages
