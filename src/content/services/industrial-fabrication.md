## Why combine fabrication with cooling equipment

A remote radiator, air cooler or heat exchanger usually needs a frame, platform or pipework to go with it. When the same team builds both, the connections, lifting points and access are planned together, and there is one point of responsibility for the job.

## What to send for a fabrication quote

1. Drawings, sketches or photos of the existing item
2. Material and finish required
3. Site location and whether erection is needed
4. Required delivery date
