## Repair, recore or replace?

| What we find | What we recommend |
|---|---|
| Dirty core, no leaks | Clean only |
| A few leaking tubes or one joint, core otherwise sound | Leak repair |
| Core blocked inside or widely corroded, tanks sound | Recore |
| Tanks or frame cracked or corroded | Full refurbishment or new radiator |
| Radiator too small for the engine or site | New radiator, sized correctly |

[verify] Final advice depends on inspection.

## Signs a radiator needs repair

1. The engine runs hotter than it used to under the same load.
2. Coolant needs regular topping up.
3. Visible green, white or rust-coloured deposits on the core or tanks.
4. Bent, missing or corroded fins over large areas.
5. Coolant in the oil, or oil in the coolant, which needs urgent checking of the engine too. [verify]
