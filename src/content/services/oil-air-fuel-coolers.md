faqs:
  - q: Why does my screw compressor trip on high temperature?
    a: The most common cause is a blocked oil cooler, either dust and oil mist on the outside or sludge inside. [verify] Other causes are low oil level, a failed thermostatic valve, a fan fault or a compressor room that is too hot; we check the cooler first because it is the most frequent fault.

  - q: Is an aftermarket cooler as good as the original part?
    a: A cooler built to the right size and fit does the same job as the original. We match the connections and mounting and size the core for the compressor's duty. [verify any performance claims against tests]
  - q: What is a fuel cooler on a generator for?
    a: A fuel cooler lowers the temperature of fuel returning from the engine to the tank. Hot return fuel can thin out and reduce engine power, and keeping it cool protects the fuel system. [verify]
  - q: How often should a compressor oil cooler be cleaned?
    a: Clean the outside whenever you see dust or oil build-up between the fins, and follow the compressor maker's service intervals. Dusty rooms such as textile mills may need much more frequent cleaning. [verify]
