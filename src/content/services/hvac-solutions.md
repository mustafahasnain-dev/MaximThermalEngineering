## Ventilation or air conditioning?

| Goal | Usually the answer |
|---|---|
| Remove heat, fumes or dust from a hall | Industrial ventilation |
| Hold a set temperature and humidity | Air conditioning with AHUs and chillers |
| Cool a process or equipment room | Ventilation first, then cooling if needed [verify] |

## What we need for an HVAC quote

1. Drawings or a sketch of the area, with ceiling height
2. The equipment to be installed, or the design you have
3. Access for ducts and pipes, and working hours allowed
4. Any shutdown windows for the plant
