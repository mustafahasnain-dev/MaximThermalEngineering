## The compressor room is part of the cooling system

An enclosed compressor depends on the room around it. [verify] If the warm air leaving the cooler cannot get out of the room, the compressor draws it back in, the cooler sees hotter air and the compressor runs closer to its limit. Before replacing a cooler, we check:

1. Whether an exhaust duct is fitted and where it discharges
2. The size of the fresh air opening
3. Room temperature next to the compressor intake on a hot afternoon
4. Other heat sources in the same room, such as dryers or other compressors
