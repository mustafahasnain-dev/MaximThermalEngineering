## Compressor heat is not only a problem

The heat a screw compressor rejects through its oil cooler is a large share of the electrical power it uses. [verify] If your plant also needs hot water for washing, process or boiler feed, a heat recovery exchanger on the oil circuit can turn some of that heat into useful energy. We can check whether it suits your plant before you replace the cooler. [verify]

## Quick check before you order

1. Is the compressor still under warranty?
2. Is the oil, filter and separator service up to date?
3. Is the room ventilated and below the maker's limit?
4. Does the temperature fall after cleaning the cooler face?
