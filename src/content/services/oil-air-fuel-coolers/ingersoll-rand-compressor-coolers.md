## Repair or replace an Ingersoll Rand cooler?

| Condition | Our usual advice |
|---|---|
| External blockage only | Deep clean, no repair needed |
| Single joint or small leak, core sound | Repair and pressure test |
| Internal fouling or sludge | Chemical clean or new core [verify] |
| Corroded or cracked core | New cooler |

## Why discharge temperature matters

A hot aftercooler outlet sends more moisture to the dryer and the air network, which can cause water in the lines and corrosion downstream. [verify] Keeping the aftercooler clean protects the rest of the air system, not only the compressor.
