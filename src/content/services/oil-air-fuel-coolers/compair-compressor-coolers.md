## Keeping an older CompAir unit running

Many older compressors are still mechanically sound, but their coolers have reached the end of their life through years of fouling and vibration. [verify] A replacement cooler built to the original fit is often the simplest way to extend the life of such a unit.

## Before replacing the cooler, check

1. Oil level and oil condition
2. Thermostatic valve operation
3. Fan rotation and motor condition
4. Room temperature and fresh air supply to the compressor
